Privacy Policy for Block Blast

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Package ID: com.aistudio.blockblast.k7m9xp

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Blast ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you play our Android puzzle game. By installing or using Block Blast, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Block Blast does not require user accounts, registration, or sign-in. Your gameplay progress and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android storage (Jetpack DataStore), including:

  • Classic Mode high score, active board progress, combo records, and games played;
  • Adventure Mode unlocked stages and star ratings;
  • In-game Gold Coin balance, booster inventory (Bombs, Hammers, Shuffles, Undos), and Sound/Haptics preferences;
  • Local purchase unlock flags and deduplicated Google Play purchase tokens for entitlements such as VIP Crown Pass and Ad-Free Arcade.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you purchase the permanent Ad-Free Arcade upgrade, Block Blast uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display interstitial advertisements between completed game sessions.

To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain technical and device signals, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID);
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general/coarse location);
  • Device model, OS version, app version, crash/performance diagnostics, and ad interaction events.
Block Blast opts out of Android Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS). You can also reset or limit your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads, or remove interstitial ads permanently via the in-game Ad-Free Arcade purchase.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its advertising services at Google Partner Sites Policy and Google Privacy Policy .

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Block Blast offers optional in-app purchases (Booster packs, Gold Coin packs, VIP Crown Pass, and Ad-Free Arcade) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing.

  • All payment credentials (credit/debit cards, UPI, carrier billing, or Google Play balance) are handled directly by Google Play.
  • We never collect, see, or store your payment card numbers or billing addresses.
  • The App receives only purchase verification tokens and product status from Google Play so items can be unlocked and non-consumable upgrades can be restored.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load AdMob advertisements and process/restore Google Play purchases.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled anytime in Settings).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google AdMob for standard ad serving and fraud prevention.

Block Blast does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, precise GPS location, or personal photo library.

5. Children's Privacy

Block Blast is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service in connection with the App, please contact us so we can help address it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because gameplay data and booster inventories are stored locally on your device, you can delete all local app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling Block Blast. Non-consumable Google Play purchases (VIP Crown Pass and Ad-Free Arcade) remain associated with your Google Play account and can be restored upon reinstall.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
